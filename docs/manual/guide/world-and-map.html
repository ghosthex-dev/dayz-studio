Chapter 3: World & Map Editing ​

DayZ Studio provides an authentic 3D environment rendered using direct ODOL mesh parsing. You can build defensive fortifications, place buildings, erase unwanted vanilla objects, and paint foliage without guessing coordinates.

🧊 The 3D Preview Viewport ​

Understanding Coordinates in DayZ ​

DayZ coordinates use three numbers [X, Y, Z]:

  • X (Red Axis): East / West position across the map.
  • Y (Green Axis): Vertical altitude (height above sea level).
  • Z (Blue Axis): North / South position.

🛠️ Precision Placement & Gizmo Modes ​

Select any placed object to activate its 3D transform gizmo:

  • Press 1 (Translation Gizmo): Move objects along X, Y, or Z axes.
  • Press 2 (Rotation Gizmo): Rotate yaw, pitch, and roll.
  • Press 3 (Scale Gizmo): Adjust prop size.

Essential Snapping Hotkeys ​

  • O (Toggle Surface Snapping): Automatically snaps objects flush to rooftops, floors, walls, and terrain contours.
  • End (Drop to Ground): Drops a floating object directly down to the terrain or surface beneath it.
  • N (Align to Terrain Slope): Automatically angles the base of your object to match the natural hill inclination.
  • G (Cycle Grid Snapping): Cycles through Off ➔ 1m ➔ 5m ➔ 10m ➔ 25m ➔ 50m.
  • Hold Shift: Performs fine rotation in 1-degree increments.
  • Hold Ctrl: Temporarily bypasses snapping for precision micro-adjustments.

✂️ The 1-Click Vanilla Object Eraser ​

Building an outpost in a dense forest or clearing an abandoned town traditionally required messy Enforce script coordinates in init.c.

In DayZ Studio, erasing vanilla objects is instantaneous:

  1. In the 3D or Map view, click on any vanilla tree, rock, wall, fence, or building.
  2. The object highlights in red.
  3. Press Delete on your keyboard (or click Remove in the Inspector).
  4. The object immediately disappears!

Behind the scenes, DayZ Studio automatically compiles an optimized removal block (DSMapEditsData) into your mod. It even recalculates the AI navigation mesh so infected and animals won't pathfind around an invisible ghost object!

🎨 Prefab Stamps & Brushes ​

Saving Prefabs ​

Have you constructed the ultimate sandbag checkpoint or survivor tent?

  1. Select your placed props.
  2. Right-click and choose Save as Prefab Stamp (.dzprefab.json).
  3. You can now stamp this complete arrangement anywhere on the map with a single click.

Painting Props with Brushes ​

Use the brush tools to organically populate environments:

  • Scatter Brush: Randomly scatters rocks, fallen trees, or debris with randomized rotation and size.
  • Line Brush: Places barricades, fences, or lighting poles in an even line between two points.
  • Ring Brush: Arranges campfires, defensive perimeters, or spikes in a circle around your base.

🔥 Avoiding Server Lag: The 150m Performance Heatmap ​

DayZ synchronizes objects across players in a 150-meter network bubble. Placing too many complex 3D meshes in one small area can cause player FPS drops and server desync.

DayZ Studio includes a live Performance Heatmap:

  • Green: Optimal performance (<150 objects per 150m bubble).
  • Yellow / Orange: Moderate density (150–350 objects).
  • Red: Warning! High object concentration. Consider reducing clutter or combining props to ensure smooth gameplay for your players.
