Chapter 4: NPC Creation & Autonomous Behaviour Graphs ​

In DayZ Studio, NPCs (Non-Player Characters) are not static mannequins. They are living characters with customizable survivor models, clothing, weapons, daily schedules, and autonomous AI brains.

👤 The NPCs Tab (Alt + 5) ​

1. Dressing & Equipping Your Character ​

  • 31 Survivor Models: Choose from diverse male and female characters.
  • 13 Outfit Presets: Instant gear presets including Survivor, Hunter, Soldier, Police Officer, Bandit, and Hazmat.
  • Custom Equipment: Equip individual hats, vests, plate carriers, backpacks, gloves, boots, and slung firearms.
  • Independent Characters: Quests pick NPCs from this tab. A single character can be involved in multiple quests or simply wander the camp as an ambient survivor.

🧠 The Behaviour Node Architecture ​

NPC logic is built using a clean, visual node flowchart:

Node TypeFunctionExamples
Events (Green)Triggers that kick off a chain of actions.Spawned, Player comes near (3m), Talked to, Hostile player seen, Time of day.
Activities (Blue)Looping behaviors that run until something interrupts them.Patrol, Wander in radius, Stand at point, Guard location, Follow player.
Steps (Yellow)Sequential actions executed in chronological order.Face player, Say text overhead, Play emote, Give item, Wait.
Flow (Gray)Conditional branch logic.Only if, Random chance.

🚶 Step-by-Step Tutorial: Creating a Guard with a Day/Night Routine ​

Let's build a camp sentry who patrols the perimeter during the daytime, warms his hands by the campfire at night, and greets approaching players:

Step 1: Create the NPC ​

  1. Switch to the NPCs Tab (Alt + 5).
  2. Click + New NPC and name him Guard_Alexei.
  3. Select the Soldier outfit preset and equip an AK-74 on his shoulder.

Step 2: Configure the Daytime Patrol ​

  1. Right-click the behavior canvas and add a Time of Day event node.
  2. Set time to 06:00 (Morning).
  3. Connect the event to a Patrol activity node.
  4. Click + Draw patrol route on the map, then click 4 waypoints around your outpost walls.

Step 3: Configure the Nighttime Routine ​

  1. Add a second Time of Day event node set to 20:00 (Night).
  2. Connect it to a Stand At activity node positioned beside your campfire.
  3. In the Inspector, set his animation state to Sit by fire (warming hands).

Step 4: Add Reactive Player Interactions ​

  1. Add a Player Comes Near event node (radius: 3 meters).
  2. Connect to Face Player.
  3. Connect to Emote: Wave.
  4. Connect to Say: "Keep your weapon holstered while inside the camp."

TIP

Always Talkable: If a player talks to Alexei while he is walking his patrol, he will smoothly pause, turn to face the player, finish the conversation or trade, and automatically resume his patrol when the player walks away!

🎭 In-Game Gestures & Emotes ​

DayZ Studio supports 19 built-in animations for NPCs:

  • Wave, Point, Salute, Come here, Nod, Clap, Shrug, Sit, Sit by the fire, Dance, and more.
  • Characters dynamically transition out of seated postures before performing gestures.
