Chapter 5: RPG Quests & Skyrim-Style Dialogue Trees ​

DayZ Studio gives your server depth and purpose through immersive, branching quests. You can create cinematic dialogue scenes, prerequisite quest chains, and objective trackers with zero scripting.

⎇ The Quests Flow Editor (Alt + 4) ​

💬 The In-Game Player Experience ​

When a player approaches a quest-giver in-game:

  1. A dynamic prompt appears: [F] Talk to NPC.
  2. Pressing F smoothly transitions the game camera into a close-up cinematic conversation perspective.
  3. Dialogue text types out smoothly in a typewriter-style animation.
  4. The player can select from 1 to 4 reply choices, ask questions, or decline the offer.

🔒 Branching Dialogue & Conditions ​

Anatomy of the Conversation Window ​

As seen in the dialogue editor above for Boris:

  • Quest State Tabs: Separate dialogue setups for Before the quest, While it is running, and After completing.
  • Dynamic Openers (Open with this line when): Automatically selects which dialogue line starts the encounter based on player conditions (e.g., if the player is bleeding, Boris can say "You're bleeding! Sit down, let me help" instead of his default greeting).
  • Player Reply Choices: Add 1 to 4 reply choices with Conditions & items gating and the accepts quest trigger checkbox.
  • Voice Line Attachment: Attach custom .ogg or .wav audio directly with the Attach voice file button.
  • Interactive Live Preview: Click through the replies in the preview box at the bottom to test dialogue branching before launching the game!

You can gate dialogue options behind specific survival criteria:

  • Held Item Check: Player must hold or carry an item (e.g., Require: BloodBagFull).
  • Health / Medical State: Option only shows if player is Bleeding, Sick, or has Health < 50%.
  • Environmental Triggers: Option only appears during Night, Rain, or Heavy Fog.
  • Faction Reputation: Option requires Friendly with Survivors (Reputation > 200).

🎙️ Adding Real Voice Lines ​

Want your characters to speak with real audio?

  1. Record your dialogue lines and save them as .ogg or .wav files.
  2. Select your dialogue node in DayZ Studio and click Attach Voice File.
  3. DayZ Studio automatically organizes the audio files into source/<mod>/sounds/ and generates the corresponding CfgSoundShaders and CfgSoundSets entries in config.cpp.

🧭 In-Game Quest Journal & Compass HUD ​

Players never have to guess what to do next:

  • Quest Journal (J): Pressing J opens an in-game quest journal displaying active objectives, lore descriptions, and completed accomplishments.
  • Compass HUD Tracking (T or Enter): Track or untrack objectives to see distance markers and directional waypoints on the compass.
  • Abandoning Quests (A twice): Double-tap A inside the journal to abandon active quests.
