Chapter 6: Economy, Custom Currency, Traders & Factions ​

Transform your server into a thriving world with trading hubs, survival economies, faction territories, and custom loot tiers.

💵 Built-in Vanilla Dollar Currency ($1 to $100) ​

DayZ Studio includes a complete physical currency system implemented as 100% vanilla inventory items:

  • DSQ_Dollar1 ($1 bill)
  • DSQ_Dollar5 ($5 bill)
  • DSQ_Dollar10 ($10 bill)
  • DSQ_Dollar20 ($20 bill)
  • DSQ_Dollar50 ($50 bill)
  • DSQ_Dollar100 ($100 bill)

Currency Highlights ​

  • Zero Mod Dependencies: Uses standard DayZ engine item mechanics.
  • Stackable & Splitable: Bills stack up to 1,000 per inventory slot and can be split into smaller denominations just like rags or ammunition.
  • Custom Bill Art: Drop your own custom .paa or .png bill textures into Documents\DayZStudio\Assets\Currency to have your server's custom currency minted automatically!

🛒 Building an NPC Trader Store ​

  1. Open the NPCs Tab (Alt + 5) and select any character.
  2. Check Enable Trader Store.
  3. In the trader inspector:
    • Click + Add Item to Catalog.
    • Select items from your game classes (e.g., M4A1, Mag_STANAG_30Rnd, CannedPeaches).
    • Set Buy Price (what players pay) and Sell Price (what the trader pays players).
    • Set stock caps and restock timers.
    • Attach standing requirements (e.g., "Requires Friendly Military Standing").

🏳️ Faction Reputation System (-1000 to +1000) ​

DayZ Studio features a multi-tiered reputation framework:

  • Survivors: Default friendly/neutral faction.
  • Military: Law-enforcement faction guarding high-tier zones.
  • Bandits: Hostile outlaws.

Automated Karma Triggers ​

  • Killing infected grants +5 Survivor Reputation.
  • Killing unarmed survivors inflicts -100 Survivor Reputation (turning players into Bandits).
  • Armed faction guards automatically raise weapons and attack players who enter their outposts with Hostile reputation!

🎒 Visual Loot Zones & Central Economy ​

Want to configure your new base to spawn high-tier snipers and tactical gear?

  1. In the Map View, switch to the Loot & Zones tool.
  2. Select your brush: Polygon, Rectangle, or Circle.
  3. Draw a perimeter around your base.
  4. Assign the loot tier: Tier 3 or Tier 4 (High Military).
  5. Studio automatically writes and updates mapgrouppos.xml and areaflags.map—eliminating the need to hand-edit thousands of lines of raw XML!
