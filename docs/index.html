NPCs with a Life of Their Own New in alpha 6

Every NPC is one character in the NPCs tab, with its own look, place, faction, trader and a behaviour graph built just like a quest. Quests pick an NPC to talk to, so one character can give several quests — and NPCs no quest uses still live their routine.

  • Events: spawned, player near, talked to, hostile seen, time of day
  • Activities: patrol, wander, stand at, follow, escort, guard
  • Steps: walk to, wait, face player, say, emote, give item
  • Day/night schedules — patrol by day, the campfire at night
  • 19 game emotes: wave, salute, point, sit by the fire…
  • Your own NPC events & steps in Enforce Script

Get Started in 3 Steps

DayZ Studio runs locally and builds its data safely from your existing Steam install.

1

Prerequisites on Steam

Ensure you have DayZ and the free DayZ Tools installed in Steam. Studio uses DayZ Tools to binarize and cryptographically sign your mods.

2

Download DayZ Studio

Download DayZStudio-Setup-*.exe from the latest release. Runs per-user with no admin rights required. All Python and Electron dependencies are included.

3

10-Min First-Time Capture

Open Studio, pick your map (Chernarus, Sakhal, etc.), and click Extract Imagery and Capture from Game. Studio safely reads heights and meshes once per map.
